## Summary

This PhD project examines how artificial intelligence can support teaching and learning in the subject Arts and Crafts at lower secondary school. It investigates how AI can become a didactic resource that supports pupils' creative processes and self-efficacy.

The project also explores how teachers use AI, the learning outcomes associated with AI-supported teaching, and how a broader understanding of art and the future development of the art world may emerge through such practice.
