## Summary

The project seeks to reframe metadata as a key step of the creative process instead of viewing it as a mere obligation. In the age of artificial intelligence, efficient creator identification has become a crucial objective for ensuring proper remuneration for artists.

By combining a literature review, a qualitative approach, and the design of practical tools, the project aims to make metadata as interesting and indispensable as a reverb, delay or compressor in the creative workflow.
