Roos van Berkel is a dancer, choreographer and movement analyst. She is a PhD Fellow at the Institute of Design at AHO and a part-time lecturer in Expanded Contemporary Dance at the Amsterdam University of the Arts. Her interdisciplinary artistic practice focuses on patterns, scores and technology.
