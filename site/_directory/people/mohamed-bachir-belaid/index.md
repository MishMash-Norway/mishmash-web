summary: 'My name is Mohamed-Bachir Belaid, a researcher in computer science, born
  in 27/06/1992.

  Between 2011 and 2016, I did my masters in computer science and operation research
  at the university of Oran in Algeria. My master thesis was on solving the multi-dimensional
  knapsack problem using a reduce and solve approach.

  Then, I started my PhD at the university of Montpellier, France in October 2016.

  My PhD work was on the use of constraint programming to solve data mining problems.

  I have defended my PhD thesis in 11/01/2020.

  In February 2020 I have joined Simula Research Laboratory in Oslo, Norway as a postdoc.
  I have worked on developing new constraint acquisition algorithms, more precisely
  on learning qualitative constraint models.

  I have worked for two months (between 08/2022 and 10/2022) as a postdoc at OsloMet
  university, where I worked on explainable AI and learning automata.

  From October 2022 to January 2023, I worked as a postdoc at NILU, the Norwegian
  "Climate and environmental research institute" to apply AI approaches for environmental
  data analysis.

  Currently and starting from February 2023, I work at NILU as AI Research Scientist.

  My main research interests are: symbolic AI, constraint reasoning and learning,
  optimization, explainable AI, concept learning, learning automata, AI applications
  on environmental problems,...etc.'
