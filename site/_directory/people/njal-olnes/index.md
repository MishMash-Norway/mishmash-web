summary: Njål Ølnes is a professor at the Department of Art and Cultural Studies,
  INN and visiting professor at NLA University College, Oslo. He holds a Ph.D. in
  Performing Art from the Norwegian Academy of Music in Oslo. From 2016-2020, Ølnes
  was a postdoctoral fellow at the Jazz Program at NTNU, where he examined the 'roles'
  one takes in improvised interaction. Ølnes is a jazz saxophonist and has used this
  as the basis for his research.Ølnes has played in Dingobats, BMX and the Trondheim
  Jazz Orchestra
