summary: "Boris Eldagsen (b. 1970) studied fine art at the Art Academies in Mainz,\
  \ Prague, and the University of Hyderabad (India). He also studied philosophy in\
  \ Cologne and Mainz. He finished both with a teaching degree.\n\nSince 2000, his\
  \ photographic and media art has been exhibited and awarded at international institutions\
  \ and festivals, including Deichtorhallen Hamburg, EMAF Osnabrück, Edinburgh Art\
  \ Festival, Noorderlicht Groningen, Singapore International Photography Festival,\
  \ Kochi-Muziris Biennale, Biennale Le Havre and Biennale of Electronic Arts Perth.\n\
  \nSince 2023, his AI-generated works have been exhibited by international galleries\
  \ in Amsterdam, Berlin, London and Panama, at festivals (ATA Zurich, AI Biennale\
  \ Essen, IPMA Kaunas, PHOTO Melbourne, ELEKTRA Digital Art Biennale Montréal, Meta.\
  \ Morf Biennale Trondheim) and in institutions and museums (MUD Foundation Miami,\
  \ La Chambre Strasbourg, California Museum of Photography, BACC Bangkok and the\
  \ Minsheng Art Museum Shanghai).\n\nSince 2004, he has taught theory of ideas and\
  \ creativity at international art colleges such as VCA Melbourne, Mainz Academy\
  \ of Fine Arts, Ludwigsburg Film Academy and Pathshala Media Institute Dhaka. He\
  \ currently teaches Generative Image AI on the ‘AI for Creatives’ Master’s programme\
  \ at LABASAD – Barcelona School for Art & Design.\nBoris is one of the few internationally\
  \ recognised experts on AI-generated images and videos and a regular guest at panel\
  \ discussions and events such as TEDx, re:publica, Medientage München, C2 Montréal\
  \ and the World AI Conference Shanghai.\n\nHis withdrawal from the Sony World Photography\
  \ Awards in April 2023 sparked a global debate on the relationship between photography\
  \ and AI-generated images. He was the “poster boy of the AI debate” (SZ) and his\
  \ work “PSEUDOMNESIA | The Electrician” became a symbol of a new era. \n\n2025 he\
  \ curated two exhibitions exploring the distinction between photography and promptography:\
  \ “RIVALS” for European Month of Photography Berlin and “PSYCHOPOMP!” for the opening\
  \ of the Roger Ballen Centre for Photography in Johannesburg, South Africa.\n\n\
  In 2025, he served as a jury member for the Ars Electronica Award in Linz, the TEZOS\
  \ Photography Award and AI Art Magazine. 2026 he was in the jury of ASVOFF Paris\
  \ and VACAT Shenzhen. \n\nHe produces a monthly AI podcast for the German Photographic\
  \ Council. He lives and works in Berlin."
