summary: Fabio Sabetta Morales is a PhD candidate at the University of Agder (Kristiansand,
  Norway) and a research fellow at the MishMash Centre for AI and Creativity. His
  research investigates the intersection of the music industries and digital technologies,
  focusing on phygital music ecosystems and the phenomenon of orchestration paralysis.
  An award-winning musician and music producer from Brazil with over two decades of
  experience as an educator, speaker, and researcher, he holds a Bachelor’s degree
  in Popular Music and a Master of Science degree in Management (Entrepreneurship
  and Innovation). Previously, he coordinated SEBRAE São Paulo’s Cultural Entrepreneur
  program and the MBA in Cultural Management and the Creative Economy at Anhembi Morumbi
  University. Committed to fostering dialogue between the Global North and the Global
  South, he serves as Scientific Director of the Centre for Studies in Business and
  Cultural and Creative Ecosystems (CENECC – Brazil), board member of the Music Tech
  Brasil Association, and coordinator for the Latin American chapter of the International
  Music Business Research Association.
