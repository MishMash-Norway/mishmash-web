summary: Sigbjørn Galåen is associate professor of 3D-graphics at Kristiania University
  College, Institute for scene, film, and media. Sigbjørn has over 20 years experience
  from the gaming industry as a 3D artist, concept artist, animator and producer,
  and he has worked both in Norwegian and international companies. From 2011 to 2016,
  Sigbjørn was co-owner and executive board member at Blink Studios, and worked on
  several projects within computer games and 3D visualization. The position also involved
  communication with external parties and securing of capital. Sigbjørn is autodidact
  within fine art, has had three solo exhibitions, and has made many cover illustrations.
  In 2024, he co-authored Norway's first text book within 3D-graphics.
