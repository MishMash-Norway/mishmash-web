summary: 'Karolina Bieszczad-Stie is an Oslo-based choreographer and artistic researcher
  with a PhD in Performing Arts from Brunel University London (2010). She is the founder
  and artistic director of Butoh Encounters, a platform for interdisciplinary artistic
  research connecting contemporary dance with science and emerging technologies.


  Her practice explores the relationships between the human body, scientific phenomena
  and technological systems, with a particular interest in unpredictability, contingency
  and moments of disruption or misalignment. Drawing on Japanese Butoh dance, choreographic
  experimentation and micro-phenomenological approaches, she explores embodied perception,
  agency and human–machine interaction.


  Her research is developed through collaborations with scientists, engineers and
  technology developers, including researchers at OsloMet, the University of Oslo''s
  Centre for Cancer Cell Reprogramming (CanCell), SINTEF and the University of Agder''s
  Motion Lab. Her choreographic work has been presented at leading performing arts
  venues, including Dansens Hus Oslo and Black Box teater, as well as in interdisciplinary
  and international contexts, including the Nobel Peace Prize Forum and the European
  Robotics Forum 2026.


  Her projects include Limit(less), exploring human–robot interaction through programmed
  contingency; Turbulent Flows, investigating the unpredictable behaviour of turbulence;
  and Call of the Void, examining a fleeting glitch between bodily response and conscious
  interpretation. Her immersive VR project NanoPlanet explores bodily perception and
  shifting scales through microscopic imagery.


  She is particularly interested in choreography as a research methodology and in
  how artistic and scientific approaches can inform one another in investigating emerging
  technologies and possible human futures.'
