This week, [Alice Demattos Guimarães](https://www.cbs.dk/en/research/departments/department-business-humanities-and-law/alice-demattos-guimaraes) will present *Where Does AI Enter the Creative Process? Mapping Artists' Uses, Choices and Creative Agency*.

## Abstract
Discussions about AI and creativity often begin with a deceptively simple question: Do artists use AI? Yet such a question says little about how AI actually enters creative practice, or what changes when it does. This presentation introduces an ongoing survey of visual artists designed to map AI use across different stages of the creative process, from ideation and experimentation to production, editing, and refinement. The survey explores where and how artists engage with AI, what they use it for, and how they perceive its implications for creative agency, authorship, originality, and control. There is an underlying broader discussion about how we might study AI not simply as a creative tool, but as part of the changing conditions of creative practice.

## Bio
Alice Demattos Guimarães is a Postdoctoral Researcher at Copenhagen Business School, where she is part of the NFR-creAIte project on transformative technologies, creativity and value creation in the cultural sector. Trained in economics and interdisciplinary social sciences, her research focuses on cultural and creative industries, with particular interests in digitalization, creative labour, authorship, cultural policy and alternative finance. Her current work examines how AI is reshaping creative practices and the relationships between creators, technologies and cultural institutions. She holds a PhD in Innovation Studies from the Western Norway University of Applied Sciences (HVL) where she continues to maintain close research collaborations.
