This week, [Tanja Bastamow](https://www.aalto.fi/en/people/tanja-bastamow) from the Department of Film at Aalto University presents *Future directions for film education at Aalto University: what we're thinking now, and what's next?*

## Abstract

This talk shares what's on our minds in Aalto's Department of Film right now in relation to the rapid development of AI tools, and how AI is entering creative practice and teaching at our department. I'll discuss the choices we face in classrooms and studios. Rather than claiming to have the answers, I will share the questions we are asking about film education and what these changes could mean over the next decade.

## Bio

Tanja Bastamow is an artist and designer with a background in scenography and production design, specialising in virtual environments and extended reality (XR). She works as a University Lecturer in Digital Design Methods in the Department of Film at Aalto University's School of Arts, Design, and Architecture. Her main areas of focus include immersive experiences, virtual production workflows, spatial storytelling, and emerging technologies. With over 20 years of experience as a designer and educator, she is also currently pursuing a doctoral degree at Aalto University, conducting research on virtual environments.
