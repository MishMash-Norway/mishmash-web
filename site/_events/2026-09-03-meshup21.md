Join us for Meshup 21, our weekly gathering for MishMashers.
This week, [Bálint Laczkó](https://balintlaczko.com/) will present. Details about his talk will be announced soon.



## Bio
[Bálint Laczkó](https://balintlaczko.com/) is an electronic music composer, music technologist, and developer in Oslo. He recently defended his PhD on Instrumental Image Sonification  as a Doctoral Research Fellow at the RITMO Centre for Interdisciplinary Studies in Rhythm, Time and Motion (UiO), where his research was situated within the interdisciplinary AUTORHYTHM project. His professional and creative practice focuses on data-driven composition, spatial 3D audio, real-time installations, computer vision, and deep learning. He holds master's degrees in Classical Composition from the Liszt Academy of Music in Budapest (2017) and Performance Technology and Electroacoustic Composition from the Norwegian Academy of Music (2021).


## Access

MishMash MeetUps are short, informal meetings in the consortium where both early career and established researchers present ongoing projects. The events are open for everyone, but, for security reasons, Zoom links are only provided to people that are affiliated with a MishMash Work Package. If not, please [ask for access](mailto:contact@mishmash.no).
