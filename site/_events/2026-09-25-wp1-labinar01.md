Welcome to the first [WP1](https://mishmash.no/wp1/) Labinar, visiting the local NMH initiative *MishMash Playground*. In this version of Playground, NMH is collaborating with ANU in Canberra.

## Abstract

*MishMash Playground* is a series of workshops held on the last Friday of each month at NMH, featuring artists, performers, and composers who explore the intersection of art and novel AI-related technology. Sessions may vary between public, live-streamed events and local, in-person gatherings. Rather than evaluating technology or diving into deep technical discussions, the focus is on its artistic use.

For this Labinar, Morten Qvenild (NMH) and Charles Martin (ANU) will play around with [IMPSY](https://charlesmartin.au/impsy-homepage/), developed by Charles. There will be a Q&A at the end, or in the chat as we go.
