Join us for an insightful discussion with [Jessica P. B. Hansen](/people/jessica-pb-hansen/) as we explore the experience of text through aesthetic reading practices.

## Abstract

An attempt at experiencing text through aesthetic reading in a world that invites efferent reading transactions and a comment on automating reading events using AI.

## Bio

Jessica P. B. Hansen is Associate Professor, Norwegian Subject in Teacher Education, at Østfold University of Applied Sciences. She is a linguist with a special focus on social interaction and technology-mediated social interaction. Currently, she is developing research projects and teaching materials related to challenges and opportunities found in the intersections between human language practices, such as those we find in interaction, play, reading, writing, etc., and machine-generated linguistic output.
