This week, [Christian Guckelsberger](/people/christian-guckelsberger/) will present *AI and Creativity: Recent, Ongoing and Future Work in Aalto University's Autotelic Interaction Research Group*.

## Abstract

In this talk, Christian Guckelsberger, Assistant Professor for Creative Technologies, provides the MishMash community with an overview of relevant work at the intersection of AI and creativity conducted within the [Autotelic Interaction Research (AIR)](http://www.autotelic.science/) group at Aalto University (Helsinki, Finland). The group and its mission will be introduced, followed by a showreel of significant past and ongoing projects with a special focus on the goals of WP7. Moving from past and present to the future, the talk concludes with plans for funded projects and new proposals.

## Bio

Christian Guckelsberger is a Computer Scientist, Art Historian and Assistant Professor in Creative Technologies at Aalto University in Helsinki, Finland. He leads the Autotelic Interaction Research (AIR) group, which seeks to understand, model and support different forms of self-directed behaviour in AI, in people, and in their interaction. His research agenda is twofold, (i) seeking to engineer artificial systems that are creative in their own right and in interaction with people, and (ii) investigating how creative AI is perceived by its users to guide sustainable AI adoption and research. Christian pursues these complementary goals through interdisciplinary, theoretical, and empirical work in various creative domains such as videogames, fine art, and design, bridging between AI, HCI, Psychology and Philosophy. Web: [autotelic.science](http://www.autotelic.science/).
