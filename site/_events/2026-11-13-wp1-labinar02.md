Welcome to the second [WP1](https://mishmash.no/wp1/) Labinar. This edition will explore Flytpunkt, a collaboration between UiO and NMH.

## Description
In this Labinar, [Victoria Johnson](https://www.hf.uio.no/imv/english/people/aca/victoriaj/) (UiO) and [Anders Tveit](https://nmh.no/en/about/staff/anders-tveit) (NMH) present Flytpunkt, an artistic research project exploring the relationship between improvisation, musical performance, and artificial intelligence.

The session will include a presentation of the work, demonstrations of the AI models that underpin it, and reflections on how Flytpunkt has evolved through different versions and informed new improvisational practices. Combining artistic demonstration and research perspectives, the Labinar offers insights into the creative possibilities that emerge when humans and AI create music together.
