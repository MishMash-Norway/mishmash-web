Research results registered under the [MishMash project in NVA](https://nva.sikt.no/projects/2744839). Do you want your result listed here? Add it to NVA and include MishMash in the funding section. See also the [research pulse](/results/pulse/) for a visual overview.

## Datasets and software

Deposits in open repositories. To be listed, deposit in the [MishMash community on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/mishmash/) or name the Research Council grant 357438 in the deposit's funding field; the list is refreshed nightly. Data can also go to [DataverseNO](https://dataverse.no/); say so at [contact@mishmash.no](mailto:contact@mishmash.no) so it can be picked up.
