The call for contributions to [AI as Environment](/events/kristiania2027/), the 2nd MishMash Conference, closes on 1 December 2026. The conference is organised together with AR@K27, the 9th Annual Artistic Research at Kristiania Symposium, and takes place at [Kristiania University of Applied Sciences](https://www.kristiania.no/en/) in Oslo on 13–14 April 2027, followed by an internal MishMash day on 15 April.

## The theme

AI as Environment approaches artificial intelligence not as a single technology but as an environment: interwoven material, energetic and algorithmic systems that shape how perception, knowledge, cultural production and action take place. The call asks for contributions that engage with one or more of three ecological registers, the infrastructural, the epistemic and the operational. The keynote speakers are Tiziana Terranova, Kyle Stine, Orit Halpern and the spring 2027 Atelier Nord/MishMash artist in residence.

## How to submit

- An abstract of at most 300 words, engaging explicitly with one or more of the three registers, and a bio of 100 words.
- Presentations are 15 minutes, as a paper, a practice-based presentation or a combination. State the preferred format in the proposal.
- The conference is in person in Oslo, and only in-person presentations are accepted. Travel and accommodation are not covered.
- Researchers at every career stage, including PhD candidates, are welcome.
- Deadline: 1 December 2026. Notification: 18 December 2026.

Proposals go through the [submission form](https://nettskjema.no/a/651115). The [full call](/assets/events/kristiania2027/hk_call-for-contributions-arak_261008_v3.pdf) and further details are on the [conference page](/events/kristiania2027/). Registration fees and practical information follow closer to the conference.
