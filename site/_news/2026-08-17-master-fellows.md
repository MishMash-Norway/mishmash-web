MishMash is opening its doors to master's students. With the new Master Fellowship programme, students at our [partner institutions](/#partners) who work on AI and creativity can become part of the MishMash community while completing their degree.

## What the fellowship offers

The fellowship is unpaid, but it comes with some clear benefits. MishMash Master Fellows will:

- be integrated fully into the activities of one or more of the [seven work packages](/about/description/#how), including their meetings and online seminars
- be eligible for funding to take part in MishMash events, such as the conferences
- be able to apply for [MishMash seed funding](/internal/funding/seed-funding/)
- be listed in the [MishMash people directory](/people/) alongside the rest of the network

Many master's students are already doing much of the exploratory work on AI and creativity at our partner institutions, and we want them to be visible and connected across the network.

## Who can apply

The programme is open to master's students at MishMash partner and affiliated institutions, in all the disciplines the centre covers: from music, film, and the performing arts to computer science, education, law, and health. A thesis project that touches on AI and creativity is the main requirement.

## How to apply

Applications are made through a [short online form](https://nettskjema.no/a/mishmash-master-fellows), where you describe your study programme, your thesis or project, and which work package(s) you would like to join. Fellows are selected by the work package leaders together with the management group.

If you have questions about the programme, [get in touch](mailto:contact@mishmash.no). Details about all MishMash fellowships are collected on the [open positions](/vacancies/#master-fellowships) page.
