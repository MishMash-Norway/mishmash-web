MishMash brings together a large group of people from numerous artistic and scientific backgrounds, working at many different types of institutions. That mixture is what the centre is for, and it works when everyone feels safe and can take part on equal terms.

Our goal is a centre that invites participation from people of all ethnicities, countries of origin, gender identities and expressions, sexual orientations, appearances, ages, abilities, economic and professional backgrounds, employment status, and beliefs. This code of conduct applies to everyone taking part, in every MishMash setting: symposia, seminars, workshops, performances, online meetings, shared channels and the code repositories.

## What we value

- **Curiosity.** We are here because the other fields in the room know something we do not. A question asked across a disciplinary border is the centre working as intended.
- **Generosity.** We share methods, data, code and contacts early, and we help newcomers find their way into the network.
- **Integrity.** We hold to high ethical standards in our research and in our daily work, and we are open about how results and works came about.
- **Care.** We look after one another, and we remember that people take part from different institutions, career stages and life situations.

## How we work together

- **Friendliness.** We treat one another as colleagues, disagree with the argument rather than the person, and assume that someone from another field has good reasons we have not heard yet.
- **Diversity.** We acknowledge and respect that colleagues bring different characteristics, backgrounds and traditions, and we treat that range as a strength.
- **Time.** We respect each other's working hours, breaks and holidays, and we agree deadlines that can be met.
- **Space and access.** We appreciate the difference between a quiet desk, an active lab and a lively social arena, and we choose venues, materials and online tools that work for people with different needs. An event says in advance what it asks of a participant.
- **Meetings.** We give meetings a clear purpose and a format that fits, and we chair them so that quiet voices are heard, not only fluent ones. Norwegian and English are both working languages, and neither decides who gets to speak.
- **Credit and consent.** We name the people whose work we build on, agree authorship early, and say what a tool contributed, following the [AI colophon](/about/ai-colophon/). We ask before a talk, performance or meeting is recorded, photographed or published, and consent can be withdrawn.

## Where the line goes

Harassment has no place in MishMash. That includes unwanted attention, intimidation, slurs, and comments that single people out by gender, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, sexuality or background, in person and online.

## If something is not right

Speak to the organiser of the activity, to a member of the [management](/about/organisation/management/), or write to [contact@mishmash.no](mailto:contact@mishmash.no). A concern is treated confidentially, and the person raising it decides how far it goes.

Anything that calls for a formal process follows the rules of the institution where it happened, and the centre helps the person find the right channel there. Nothing on this page replaces an employer's or a university's own procedures, or the law.

## How this page is written

The code is written with the members, not for them. It is discussed at the centre's symposia and revised as the network learns what it needs. Suggestions are welcome at [contact@mishmash.no](mailto:contact@mishmash.no) or through [suggest a change](https://github.com/MishMash-Norway/mishmash-web/edit/main/site/about/code-of-conduct/index.md).
