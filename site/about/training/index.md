Education is one of the centre's main tasks. MishMash trains doctoral and postdoctoral fellows, students, and professionals who finished their studies long ago.

## Doctoral fellows

Every academic partner institution in the centre has at least one doctoral fellow connected to MishMash, and several have more. They work in a work package but belong to the whole network, so that a fellow in music technology and a fellow in law meet questions they would not meet at their own institution.

Fellows take part in every centre activity, and the centre's own courses are designed for them.

## Micro courses

Each symposium carries for-credit micro courses on specific methods and topics, taught by people in the network. They are meant first for doctoral fellows, and are open to advanced students, postdoctoral fellows and anyone who wants concentrated input on a method.

The symposia, and the courses with them, rotate between institutions, so a fellow does not have to be at a large university to take them.

## Students

Hundreds of students at the partner institutions are involved in MishMash through courses, projects and theses. Students are treated as partners rather than an audience: they bring a fresh view of tools that older researchers learned late, they are often the first to use what the centre builds, and they take technical know-how and critical questions into working life.

The [lab](/lab/) and the student interfaces at [mishmash.no/ui/](/ui/) are built by students, in the open.

## Lifelong learning

Not everyone who needs to understand creative AI is at a university. The centre runs workshops for creative professionals, educators and technologists, many of them together with local organisations that already know those communities. The sessions are hands-on: people try co-creative tools rather than hear about them. The aim is to avoid a lag of a generation in what people can do with AI.

## After MishMash

The ambition is to apply for a national research school in this field, so that the training continues after the centre ends. Until then, the training takes place in the symposia and the micro courses.
