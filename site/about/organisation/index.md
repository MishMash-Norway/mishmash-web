MishMash is governed and managed by several bodies, each with its own responsibility and its own rhythm:

| Body | What it does | How often it meets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Management Group](management/) | Day-to-day operation of the centre | Weekly |
| [Work Package Leader Group](wp-leaders/) | Progress and planning across the seven work packages | Biweekly |
| [Board](board/) | Decisions on operation, budget, and strategic development | Twice a semester |
| [Council](council/) | Advice from each budget partner institution on the centre's operation | Once a semester |
| [Scientific Advisory Board](scientific-advisory-board/) | International advice on activities and priorities | Once a year |
| [Stakeholder Board](stakeholder-board/) | Advice from public and private organisations | Once a year |

Alongside the formal bodies, the centre meets continuously: weekly lunch seminars for everyone, work package meetings every second week, irregular meetings on each of the three perspectives (machines, humans and society), physical conferences twice a year.

Everyone in MishMash is expected to follow the [code of conduct](/about/code-of-conduct/).
