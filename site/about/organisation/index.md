MishMash is governed and managed by several bodies, each with its own responsibility and its own rhythm:

| Body | What it does | How often it meets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Management](management/) | Day-to-day operation of the centre | Weekly |
| [Work Package Leader Group](wp-leaders/) | Academic progress and planning across the seven work packages | Monthly |
| [Board](board/) | Decisions on operation, budget, appointments and strategic development | Twice a semester |
| [Council](council/) | Advice from each academic partner institution on the centre's operation | Once a semester |
| [Scientific Advisory Board](scientific-advisory-board/) | International advice on activities and priorities | Once a year |
| [Stakeholder Board](stakeholder-board/) | Advice from public and private organisations, with the work package leaders | Once a year |

The Board has seven members: a leader from an institution other than the host, two faculty members, one administrator, one temporary member, one student and one representative of industry. The Centre Manager attends as an observer, and the Head of Administration serves as secretary. The Council has one representative from each academic partner institution, most of them in a leadership position at their own institution. Members of the Scientific Advisory Board are also invited to Norway to give guest lectures and workshops.

Alongside the formal bodies, the centre meets continuously: weekly lunch seminars for everyone, work package meetings every second week, and monthly meetings on each of the three perspectives, machines, humans and society. How members are expected to treat each other is set out in the [code of conduct](/about/code-of-conduct/).
