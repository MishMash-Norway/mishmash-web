MishMash is organised through several bodies, each with its own responsibility and its own rhythm:

| Body | What | How often |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Management Group](management/) | Day-to-day operation of the centre | Weekly |
| [Work Package Leader Group](wp-leaders/) | Progress and planning across the seven work packages | Biweekly |
| [Board](board/) | Overall decisions on operation, budget and strategic development | Twice a semester |
| [Council](council/) | Advice from each budget partner institution on the running of the centre | Once a semester |
| [Scientific Advisory Board](scientific-advisory-board/) | Advice on activities and priorities from international experts | Once a year |
| [Stakeholder Board](stakeholder-board/) | Advice from public and private organisations | Once a year |

The centre also runs a range of activities for its members: weekly lunch seminars for everyone, work package meetings every second week, ad hoc meetings on the three perspectives (machines, humans and society), and physical gatherings twice a year.

Everyone in MishMash is expected to follow the [code of conduct](/about/code-of-conduct/).
