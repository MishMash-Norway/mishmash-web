A one-day workshop for the work package leaders and co-leads. It runs alongside the [recruit workshop](../2027-01-25-recruit-workshop/), and the two share the morning sessions, lunch and dinner.

- Date: Monday 25 January 2027, 09.00 to 16.00, dinner at 17.00
- Place: Faculty of Humanities, University of Oslo, 12th floor. Online participation is possible.
- Registration: in Nettskjema, by 15 January 2027
- Before the workshop: send in three goals for your work package for 2027, by 15 January

## Programme

| Time | Session |
| --- | --- |
| 09.00 | Welcome; HPC; data management (joint with the recruits) |
| 10.00 | AI Use Case Challenge and AI guidelines (joint with the recruits) |
| 12.00 | Lunch |
| 13.00 | Vision statements |
| 14.45 | Roles: who does what |
| 15.45 | What we take with us (joint) |
| 17.00 | Dinner |

Questions about travel and registration go to the administrative coordinator, [Eskil Muan Sæther](/people/eskil-muan-saether/).
