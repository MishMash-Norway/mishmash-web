A one-day workshop for the recruits in MishMash: PhD and postdoctoral fellows funded by the Research Council, those funded by their own institution, and others who take part fully in the centre. It runs alongside the [WP leader workshop](../2027-01-25-wpl-workshop/), and the two share the morning sessions, lunch and dinner.

- Date: Monday 25 January 2027, 09.00 to 16.00, dinner at 17.00
- Place: Faculty of Humanities, University of Oslo, 12th floor. Online participation is possible.
- Registration: in Nettskjema, by 15 January 2027

## Programme

| Time | Session |
| --- | --- |
| 09.00 | Welcome; HPC; data management (joint with the WP leaders) |
| 10.00 | AI Use Case Challenge and AI guidelines (joint with the WP leaders) |
| 12.00 | Lunch |
| 13.00 | Code of conduct |
| 13.45 | Working together |
| 14.45 | Your own pages on mishmash.no |
| 15.45 | What we take with us (joint) |
| 17.00 | Dinner |

Questions about travel and registration go to the administrative coordinator, [Eskil Muan Sæther](/people/eskil-muan-saether/).
