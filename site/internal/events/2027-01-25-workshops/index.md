Two parallel workshops, one for the work package leaders and co-leads and one for the recruits (PhD and postdoctoral fellows), with joint sessions in the morning, a shared lunch and a shared dinner.

- Date: Monday 25 January 2027, 09.00 to 16.00, dinner at 17.00
- Place: Faculty of Humanities, University of Oslo, 12th floor. Online participation is possible.
- Registration: in Nettskjema, by 15 January 2027
- Who: the WP leaders and co-leads, and all recruits: those funded by the Research Council, those funded by their own institution, and others who take part fully in MishMash

## Programme

| Time | WP leaders | Recruits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 09.00 | Welcome; computing and machine learning; data management (joint) | Joint |
| 10.00 | AI Use Case Challenge and AI guidelines (joint) | Joint |

| 14.45 | Roles: who does what | Your own pages on mishmash.no |
