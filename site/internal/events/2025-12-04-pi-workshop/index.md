A strategy workshop for the work package leaders and the principal investigators among the members, in Sem 4, ZEB, University of Oslo, 09.00 to 16.00. The evening before, the public concert [Quantum Loops, Broken Symmetries](/events/quantum-loops-broken-symmetries/) was followed by a reception.

## Programme

- Information from the management and the work package leaders
- What will we work on?
- How should we work?
- Web pages and communication
- Obtaining more funding
- General discussion
