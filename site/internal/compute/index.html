Computing resources

This page shows where MishMash members can get computing power for their AI work. The centre has some centrally managed resources and its own projects. In addition, several institutions have their own computing resources. Choose your institution below to see what you can use, what you can apply for, and whom to ask.

National services

The national systems are run by Sigma2, which provides computing and storage for research, and the Norwegian AI Cloud, which adds AI capacity and training.
