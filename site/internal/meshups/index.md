General structure for Thursday MeshUps:

- 11:50-12:00: Tech test
- 12:00-12:02: Wait for people to arrive
- 12:02-12:04: Alexander gives a few messages
- 12:04-12:05: Moderator briefly introduces the speaker
- 12:05-12:20: Presentation
- 12:20-12:29: Moderator leads the Q&A session
- 12:29-12:30: Alexander closes the session

## Why we do not record

MeshUps are not recorded, and the slides are not shared afterwards. We want the meetings to be synchronous, alive and live, with the feeling that some things in life actually pass. They are also a social meeting point in the centre. Speakers can be told this when they are invited, so they can speak freely and show work in progress.
